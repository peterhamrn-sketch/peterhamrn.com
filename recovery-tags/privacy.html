Recovery Tags Privacy Policy

Last updated October 4, 2026.

Recovery Tags by Peter Ham Solutions uses the information you provide to activate and manage your tags and to provide the contact options you choose for someone who finds your item.

Information we use

This may include your name, email address, phone number, tag and item information, and technical information needed for security and verification.

Text-message verification

If you provide a mobile number and request a verification code, we may use that number to send one-time or transactional authentication messages related to Recovery Tags. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on your requests. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Mobile information and SMS consent are not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Service providers may process information only as needed to deliver and secure the service.

Your recovery page

You choose which contact methods a finder can use. Recovery Tags displays only the contact options you select.

Contact

Questions about privacy or Recovery Tags can be sent to recovery@peterhamrn.com.
