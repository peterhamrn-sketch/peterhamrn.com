It Started With a “What If?”

One day, while browsing Facebook, I noticed several posts that sounded awfully familiar:

“Hey, I found a set of motorcycle keys at [location]. Does anybody know who they belong to? I’d love to get them back to their owner.”

And that got me thinking…

What if…

What if those keys had a simple tag attached?

What if whoever found them could just tap the tag with their phone and immediately know how to contact the owner?

No searching through Facebook posts. No hoping somebody recognizes the keys. No special app to download.

Just a simple way to help lost belongings find their way home.

And that’s how Recovery Tags began.

Privacy Policy · Terms & SMS Terms

Not a GPS Tracker

Recovery Tags aren’t tracking devices. They don’t show an item’s live location or transmit GPS coordinates.

Instead, they make it easy for someone who finds your belongings to contact you.

They can complement a GPS or Bluetooth tracker, but they serve a different purpose.
