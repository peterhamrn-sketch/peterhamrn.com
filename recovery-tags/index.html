Recovery Tags

A simple tap can help your lost stuff find its way home.

Attach a Recovery Tag to your keys, bag, luggage, or another item you don't want to lose. If someone finds it, they can tap the tag with their phone and instantly see the contact options you've chosen to share—no special app required.

1 · Attach

Put your Recovery Tag on the item you want to protect.

2 · Activate

Register the tag and choose how a finder can reach you.

3 · Get it back

A finder taps the tag and can call, text, or email you using your selected options.
