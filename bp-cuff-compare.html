BP Compare

Compare paired upper-arm and wrist blood-pressure readings and see how the cuffs differ over time.

Version 1.0 · Waiting for Review

See the difference in every paired reading

BP Compare records both cuffs and shows the actual differences over time. In Build 8, the individual cuff-difference chart is the primary comparison view; cumulative trends provide a secondary view of the running average.

1

Record paired readings

Enter upper-arm and wrist systolic, diastolic, and pulse readings together. Record the arm/wrist side and required device information; notes are optional.

2

Compare individual differences

The primary chart shows the actual cuff difference for each paired reading: wrist minus upper arm. Positive values mean the wrist reading was higher; negative values mean it was lower.

3

Review cumulative trends

Use the secondary cumulative cuff-difference chart to see the running average over time alongside the individual reading comparisons.

4

Manage patients and history

Create and manage patients, then filter History by patient. Select a reading to edit, or select one or multiple readings to delete with confirmation.

5

Sync with iCloud

Keep data synchronized across your Apple devices with iCloud and CloudKit. Check the sync-status indicator and open Sync Details to review synchronization information.

6

Import and export CSV

Use Manage to import compatible BP Compare CSV files or export your data for use outside the app.
