Blood Pressure Comparison Tool

BP Cuff Compare

A focused application for recording simultaneous blood-pressure readings from two devices and seeing the difference between them over time.

Working project and study tool

Compare readings without doing the math by hand

BP Cuff Compare was created to study how closely an arm cuff and wrist cuff agree across repeated readings. It keeps the measurements, calculates the differences, and makes longer-term patterns easier to see.

1

Record both cuffs

Enter simultaneous systolic, diastolic, and pulse readings from the two devices in one place.

2

Calculate deviation

See the difference between the arm and wrist readings immediately instead of calculating it manually.

3

Review cumulative data

Keep all saved readings together and review averages, variation, and overall comparison results.

4

Graph the pattern

Use a visual history to see whether the devices remain consistent or drift apart over time.

5

Work offline

Save readings when internet access is unavailable and continue the comparison without interrupting the study.

6

Keep the purpose focused

The application is a comparison and study tool, not a diagnostic system or substitute for clinical judgment.
