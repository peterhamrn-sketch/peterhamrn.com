Native iPhone App

Availability

Turn a busy calendar into clear time windows you can review and share—without manually comparing every appointment.

Current working build

Useful availability, not just empty space

Availability reads the calendars you select directly from your iPhone, accounts for the time needed around scheduled events, and prepares a clean message with the windows that are genuinely useful.

This week or next week

This Week runs from the current time through Friday. Next Week covers Monday through Friday.

Travel-aware results

Event locations, the chosen travel mode, and the travel buffer help protect the time needed between commitments.

Clean time windows

Displayed windows are rounded to the nearest five minutes, and windows shorter than 60 minutes are left out.

Review before sending

The app prepares a Messages draft for review. Sending can remain manual or be automated if desired.

Made to fit your schedule

  • Choose which iPhone calendars are included.
  • Set the recipient and home location.
  • Configure working hours, travel mode, and travel buffer.
  • Use the resulting draft as the final review point.

Requirements & compatibility

Designed as a native iPhone app. It uses calendars and event locations available on the user’s iPhone and prepares its result for Apple Messages.
